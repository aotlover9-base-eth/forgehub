# Code of Conduct

ForgeHub is a small project with a simple standard: be the
kind of person others want to build with.

## Expected

- Be respectful and constructive - critique code and ideas,
  not people.
- Assume good faith; ask before concluding.
- Keep discussions on-topic and welcoming to newcomers.
- Accept maintainer decisions gracefully; appeal with new
  information, not repetition.

## Not acceptable

- Harassment, insults, or discrimination of any kind.
- Publishing other people's private information.
- Deliberately disruptive behaviour in issues, PRs or
  community spaces.
- Using project spaces to distribute malicious snippets or
  to solicit credentials - this project exists to make
  running shared commands *safer*, and abusing its trust
  model is the fastest way to be banned.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions, close threads,
and ban participants who violate this code. Report problems
privately to the maintainer (see the contact route in
`SECURITY.md`); reports are handled confidentially.

Adapted in spirit from the Contributor Covenant (v2.1).
